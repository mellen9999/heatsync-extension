reading chat needs no account

  1. open a stream

    open any twitch, kick or youtube stream. heatsync chat appears by itself

    twitch.tv kick.com youtube.com/live

    a short tour shows up the first time. skip it any time

  2. hide and show the chat

    the hide button in the chat's tab bar tucks it away. click the thin white strip at the edge of the page to bring it back

  3. sign in (optional)

    an account lets you send to twitch, kick and youtube at once, and use your own emotes on every device
